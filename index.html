Protecting Car Buyers & Empowering Sellers

Smart, actionable advice to help everyday citizens make safe financial decisions

5 Essential Checks Before Buying

  • RC & Insurance Verification: Verify owner name on Vahan portal and check active NCB bonus.
  • Accident & Flood History: Inspect engine bay bolts for tampering and under-seat foam for water stains.
  • Service Record History: Always ask for authorized dealership service logs to confirm odometer mileage.
  • Comprehensive Test Drive: Test clutch feedback, suspension noises over speedbumps, and AC cooling performance.
  • Transfer Formalities: Ensure Form 29 & 30 are signed to safely complete ownership transfer.

How Sellers Can Get Maximum Value

  • Professional Detailing: A clean car exterior and deep-cleaned interior can increase offer price by 5-10%.
  • Fix Minor Scratches: Minor paint touch-ups prevent buyers from demanding aggressive price discounts.
  • Gather Maintenance Bills: Documented oil changes and battery replacements build strong buyer trust.
  • High-Quality Photos: Take clear daylight photos of exterior, dashboard, seats, and tires.
  • Know Your Bottom Line: Use AutoValuate fair range so you don't accept lowball dealership quotes.

Project Reflection & Technical Overview

Deployment experience, integration details, and societal impact statement

Societal Impact & Mission

Empowering Everyday Consumers

Buying or selling a car is one of the largest financial transactions for middle-class families. Used car markets often suffer from information asymmetry, where dealers exploit uninformed buyers or underquote sellers.

This interactive platform bridges that gap by delivering objective, data-backed car valuations free of charge, protecting citizens from unfair deals.

Deployment & Web Integration

Architecture & Public Hosting

The system features a Dual-Engine Architecture:

  • Zero-Latency Static Client Engine: Evaluates decision rules in JavaScript (ml_engine.js) directly in the browser, making the site deployable on Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages.
  • Flask Python REST API: app.py provides backend model evaluation for hosting on Render / Railway.

Challenges Overcome & Future Improvements

Engineering Learnings & Roadmap

User Experience vs ML Complexity

Removed technical machine learning metrics (R² charts, tree nodes) from the primary consumer flow to maintain a clean, friendly product focused on fair market prices and actionable consumer advice.

Regional & City Tax Variations

Future versions will incorporate state RTO taxes (e.g. Karnataka vs Delhi vs Maharashtra) to adjust price estimates based on location demand.
